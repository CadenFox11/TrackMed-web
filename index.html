Frequently Asked Questions

  • Where is my health and dose data stored?
    All dose logs, schedules, and biometric settings stay strictly on your iPhone. TrackMed has no user accounts, no cloud health storage, and no third-party tracking.
  • How do I back up or move my history to a new iPhone?
    Open the Profile tab and tap Backup History (JSON) to save a local backup file, then use Restore from Backup (JSON) on your new device. You can also export a spreadsheet using Export Dose History (CSV).
  • Why don't my lock-screen reminders show the medication name?
    For your privacy, Private Lock-Screen Reminders are enabled by default. You can customize this anytime under Profile → Notifications & Privacy.
  • Where do the pharmacokinetic curves and interaction notes come from?
    Parameters are compiled from public-domain clinical references including FDA DailyMed prescribing labels, NIH RxNorm, NIH Office of Dietary Supplements (ODS), and peer-reviewed PubMed literature.
