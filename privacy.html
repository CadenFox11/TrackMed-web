Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 21, 2026

1. Summary: Your Data Stays on Your Device

TrackMed is designed from the ground up for privacy. We do not require an account, we do not operate a cloud server to store your health information, and we do not collect, sell, rent, or share your personal or medical data.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your iPhone

When you use TrackMed, the following information is stored strictly on your device using Apple's local SwiftData and UserDefaults storage:

  • Dose Logs & Schedules: Substance names, amounts, timestamps, routes, food flags, and daily schedule preferences you enter.
  • Optional Biometric Calibration: Biological sex, weight, height, age, sleep window, clearance speed preference, and nicotine status used solely to calculate on-device pharmacokinetic estimates.
  • Custom Compounds: Any custom entries you create inside My Stack.

TrackMed does not sync your health or medication logs to iCloud or any external server.

3. Local Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, TrackMed schedules notifications locally on your iPhone using Apple's UNUserNotificationCenter. By default, Private Lock-Screen Reminders are enabled so medication names and doses are not displayed on your Lock Screen. You may disable or change notification preferences anytime in Profile or iOS Settings.

4. Analytics, Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

TrackMed contains no third-party advertising networks, tracking pixels, behavioral analytics SDKs, or data brokers.

5. Data Export, Backup & Deletion

You remain in full control of your data at all times:

  • Export & Backup: You may voluntarily export your history as a CSV or JSON file using the iOS share sheet or Files app. Once exported, that file is governed by where you choose to save or send it.
  • Deletion: You can delete individual logs inside the app, or remove all locally stored TrackMed data permanently by deleting the app from your iPhone.

6. Children's Privacy

TrackMed is intended for users aged 17 and older and is not directed to children under 13. Because we do not collect personal data from any user, we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

If future versions of TrackMed introduce optional cloud features, HealthKit integration, or other capabilities that change how data is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are released, with a revised "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
